Notes — Vitruvian Orders, Entasis & Enveloped-Sphere 3D Contours
================================================================

1. The 60-Station Twisted Column and Classical Orders in the North
------------------------------------------------------------------

Book III opens Dürer's architectural treatise with an homage to :person:`Vitruvius <vitruvius>` (:cite:`vitruvius:architectura`), balanced by the German master's flair for bold ornamental invention. To define the sinuous three-dimensional contour of a twisted column (Figs. 9–10), Dürer conceives every station along its coiled helical axis as the center of an inscribed sphere (*ein yeklicher punct in der gewunden axt der seulen ein centrum einer kugel*)—an early practical instance of envelope theory in spatial constructive geometry.
