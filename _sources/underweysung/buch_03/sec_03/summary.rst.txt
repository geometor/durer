Summary — Roman *Antiqua* Capitals & Modular Gothic *Textura*
=============================================================

3. **Constructive Typography: Roman *Antiqua* & Modular *Textura* (pp. 115–141)**:

   - Constructs ``A``–``Z`` in the :math:`10 \times 10` (and :math:`9 \times 9`) *rechte Fierung* with :math:`1 : \frac{1}{3}` stroke contrast, tangent circular serif fillets (*Ausschweiffung*), and two-circle
     shifted-diagonal bowls (``O, Q, C, D, G``).
   - Builds Gothic *Textura* minuscules combinatorially from the foundational ``i``-stem (3 unit
     squares + 2 :math:`45^\circ` *Ort-Fierungen*), proving the :math:`\sqrt{2}` stem pitch of ``n`` and ``m``.
