===========================================================
Bilingual Technical Glossary (1525 German • 1532 Latin)
===========================================================

.. _term-abstelen:

Abstelen (*Transfer by Orthogonal Projection*)
==============================================
Dürer's workshop verb in Books I, III, and IV ("to steal off") for transferring coordinates and
chord widths between elevation (*aufgezogner Grund*) and ground plan (*nidergedruckter Grund*)
via perpendicular projectors.

.. _term-aufgezogner-grund:

Aufgezogner Grund (*Orthographia / Elevation*)
==============================================
The upright vertical elevation view coupled to the horizontal ground plan (*nidergedruckter
Grund*) in Dürer's two-view descriptive geometry.

.. _term-augpunct:

Aug / Gesichtspunct (*Oculus / Eye & Principal Vanishing Point*)
================================================================
In Book IV perspective, both the 3D station point of the beholder's eye and its orthogonal foot
on the transparent picture plane toward which lines perpendicular to the frame converge.

.. _term-ausschweiffung:

Ausschweiffung (*Circular Serif Fillet*)
========================================
Compass-drawn serif bracket tangent to both a Roman capital's stem and the framing square's edge
(Book III, pp. 115–130; e.g., radius $\frac{1}{7}$ of the square side on the outer feet of ``A``).

.. _term-barlini:

Barlini / Parrlini (*Linea parallela / Parallel Line*)
======================================================
Dürer's vernacular coinage (Book I, Fig. 2) for straight or curved equidistant parallel lines.

.. _term-brennspiegellini:

Brennspiegellini / Brennlini (*Parabola / Burning-Mirror Line*)
===============================================================
Dürer's German term (Book I, Figs. 35–36) for Apollonius's parabola, sliced parallel to a cone
generator and used immediately to focus parallel solar rays in a burning mirror.

.. _term-corpora-regularia:

Corpora regularia (*Regular & Semi-Regular Polyhedra*)
======================================================
The five Platonic and seven Archimedean solid bodies unfolded into planar nets in Book IV
(Figs. 29–41).

.. _term-demonstrative:

Demonstrative vs. Mechanice
===========================
Dürer's explicit epistemological distinction in Book II between exact deductive Euclidean
constructions (*demonstrative*) and practical craftsman approximations (*mechanice*).

.. _term-durchsichtige-abschneydung:

Durchsichtige Abschneydung (*Planar Section of the Visual Pyramid*)
===================================================================
The transparent glass picture plane slicing the rectilinear visual rays (*Streimlinien*) between
the 3D object and the eye (Book IV, pp. 173–181).

.. _term-eierlini:

Eierlini (*Ellipsis / Egg-Line*)
================================
Dürer's German coinage (Book I, Fig. 34) for the conic ellipse. While Dürer's woodcut narrows
slightly toward the cone apex like a hen's egg, his own 12-station orthographic transfer formula
$w_k = \frac{\sqrt{2k(12-k)}}{3}$ proves exact bilateral symmetry ($w_{12-k} = w_k$).

.. _term-fierung:

Rechte Fierung & Ort-Fierung (*Square & 45° Diamond Module*)
============================================================
The upright square framing Roman *Antiqua* capitals ($10 \times 10$ or $9 \times 9$) and the
stacked unit squares + $45^\circ$ diamond caps generating Gothic *Textura* minuscules (Book III).

.. _term-fischblosen:

Fischblosen (*Vesica Piscis / Fish-Bladder Lens*)
=================================================
Symmetric lens of two intersecting congruent circles used in Book II, Fig. 18 to construct the
workshop nonagon ($9$-gon).

.. _term-gabellini:

Gabellini (*Hyperbola / Fork-Line*)
===================================
Dürer's German coinage (Book I, Fig. 37) for the conic hyperbola cut parallel to the cone's axis.

.. _term-muschellini:

Muschellini (*Linea conchois / Conchoid of Nicomedes*)
======================================================
The "mussel-shell line" $r = b + a\sec\theta$ constructed pointwise and mechanically in Book I,
Figs. 31–33.

.. _term-nidergedruckter-grund:

Nidergedruckter Grund (*Ichnographia / Ground Plan*)
====================================================
The top-down horizontal ground plan paired below the upright elevation (*aufgezogner Grund*).

.. _term-ortstrich:

Ortstrich / Diameter (*Diagonal or 45° Mitre Line*)
===================================================
Corner-to-corner square diagonal governing Book II circle quadrature, Book III shifted-circle
bowls (``O``, ``Q``, ``C``, ``D``), and *Textura* mitre cuts.

.. _term-punct:

Punct (*Punctum / Point*)
=========================
Book I's opening Euclidean definition: that which has neither magnitude, length, breadth, nor
thickness, yet is the beginning and end of all bodily things.

.. _term-richtscheyt:

Richtscheyt (*Regula / Straightedge*)
=====================================
Unmarked straightedge paired with the compass (*Zirckel*).

.. _term-schlangenlini:

Schlangenlini (*Linea serpentina / Serpentine S-Curve*)
=======================================================
Inflected doubly-curved line introduced alongside straight and circular lines at the opening of
Book I (Fig. 1).

.. _term-schneckenlini:

Schneckenlini (*Cochlis / Planar Spiral & Volute*)
==================================================
Archimedean and projective planar spirals constructed across 12 angular sectors in Book I
(Figs. 7–11, 17–24).

.. _term-schrauffenlini:

Schrauffenlini (*Helix / Screw-Thread Spatial Helix*)
=====================================================
Cylindrical and conical spatial helices projected in plan and elevation (Book I, Figs. 12–15).

.. _term-winckelmess:

Winckelmeß (*Norma / Carpenter's Right-Angle Square*)
=====================================================
Right-angle square used both for perpendiculars and, with a sliding bar, in Plato's mechanical
cube-duplicating instrument (Book IV, Fig. 50).

.. _term-zirckel:

Zirckel & Unverruckter Zirckel (*Compass & Fixed-Aperture Compass*)
===================================================================
Divider compass, including single-aperture ("rusty compass") constructions *mit unverrucktem
Zirckel* (Book II, Fig. 16).
