Notes — Plane Enclosure, Corners vs. Angles & Quadrilaterals (Figs. 1–8)
========================================================================

1. Distinction Between Exterior Corner (*Eck*) and Interior Angle (*Winckel*)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

In Book II (Figs. 1–8), Dürer emphasizes the practical craftsman's operational distinction between looking upon the sharp external vertex (*Eck*, salient angle) versus looking into the recessed hollow (*Winckel*, interior cavity). He grounds angle construction in Thales's semicircle theorem (Fig. 4) and classifies eight species of quadrilaterals (*Fierungen*)—distinguishing the true square (:term:`rechte Fierung <fierung>`) from oblongs, rhombi, rhomboids, and four empirical trapezia.
