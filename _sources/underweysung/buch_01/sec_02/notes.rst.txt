Notes — Point, Elementary Lines & Parallel Curves (Figs. 1–5)
==============================================================

1. Euclidean Foundations and German Geometric Nomenclature
----------------------------------------------------------

In translating :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` *Elements* Book I definitions (:cite:`euclid:elements`) into German print for the first time, Dürer invents a precise vernacular technical vocabulary: :term:`Punct <punct>`, *gerade Lini*, *Zirckellini*, and equidistant :term:`Barlinien <barlini>`. Crucially, Dürer augments Euclid's planar palette with the inflected :term:`Schlangenlini <schlangenlini>` (serpentine S-curve) and observes the physical convergence of plumb lines toward the center of the earth—grounding abstract geometry directly in workshop observation.
