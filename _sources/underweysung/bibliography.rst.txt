Primary Editions of Dürer
=========================

.. _cite-durer-1525:

- **Dürer, Albrecht** (1525). *Underweysung der Messung, mit dem Zirckel un[d] Richtscheyt, in
  Linien Ebnen unnd gantzen Corporen, durch Albrecht Dürer zu sammen getzoge[n], und zu nutz
  alle[n] kunstliebhabenden mit zu gehörigen figuren, in truck gebracht, im jar. M.D.XXV.*
  Nuremberg: [Hieronymus Andreae]. Copy-text witness: SLUB Dresden, ``PPN 27778509X`` (`S.B. 618`),
  collated with the twice-proofread diplomatic edition on German Wikisource.

.. _cite-durer-1528:

- **Dürer, Albrecht** (1528). *Hierinn sind begriffen vier Bücher von menschlicher Proportion.*
  Nuremberg: Hieronymus Formschneider.

.. _cite-durer-1532-camerarius:

- **Dürer, Albrecht**, trans. **Joachim Camerarius** (1532). *Alberti Dureri Clarissimi Pictoris
  et Geometrae Institutionum Geometricarum Libri Quatuor.* Paris: Christian Wechel (reprinted
  1535; Arnhem, 1606).

.. _cite-durer-1538:

- **Dürer, Albrecht** (1538). *Underweysung der Messung, mit dem Zirckel und Richtscheyt, in
  Linien Ebnen unnd gantzen Corporen* (2nd enlarged posthumous edition). Nuremberg: Hieronymus
  Formschneider.

Classical, Medieval & Renaissance Sources
=========================================

.. _cite-abulwafa-craftsmen:

- **Abū al-Wafāʾ al-Būzjānī** (c. 990). *Kitāb fīmā yaḥtāju ilayhi al-ṣāniʿ min aʿmāl al-handasa*
  (*On Those Parts of Geometry Needed by Craftsmen*). Baghdad.

.. _cite-alberti-1435:

- **Alberti, Leon Battista** (1435–1436). *De pictura / Della pittura.* Florence.

.. _cite-apollonius-conics:

- **Apollonius of Perga** (c. 200 BC). *Conics (Konika, Books I–VIII).* Alexandria / Pergamon.

.. _cite-archimedes-spirals:

- **Archimedes of Syracuse** (c. 225 BC). *On Spirals (Peri helikon)* and *Measurement of a
  Circle (Kyklou metresis).* Syracuse.

.. _cite-euclid-elements:

- **Euclid of Alexandria** (1505 / 1908). *Elements (Stoicheia, Books I–XIII).* Latin edition by
  Bartolomeo Zamberti (Venice: Joannes Tacuinus, 1505 — owned by Dürer in 1507); English scholarly
  edition by T. L. Heath (Cambridge University Press, 1908; GEOMETOR ``euclid`` / ``elements``).

.. _cite-kepler-1604:

- **Kepler, Johannes** (1604). *Ad Vitellionem Paralipomena, Quibus Astronomiae Pars Optica
  Traditur*, Cap. IV (*De Coni sectionibus*). Frankfurt: Claudius Marnius.

.. _cite-kepler-1619:

- **Kepler, Johannes** (1619). *Harmonices Mundi Libri V*, Liber II (*De Congruentia Figurarum
  Harmonicarum*). Linz: Johann Planck.

.. _cite-pacioli-1509:

- **Pacioli, Luca**, illus. **Leonardo da Vinci** (1509). *Divina Proportione: Opera a tutti
  gl'ingegni perspicaci e curiosi necessaria.* Venice: Paganino de' Paganini (GEOMETOR ``pacioli``).

.. _cite-pappus-synagoge:

- **Pappus of Alexandria** (c. 340 AD). *Mathematical Collection (Synagoge, Books III, V & VII).*
  Alexandria (GEOMETOR ``pappus``).

.. _cite-plato-timaeus:

- **Plato** (c. 360 BC). *Timaeus* (53c–56c). Athens (GEOMETOR ``plato``).

.. _cite-roritzer-1487:

- **Roriczer, Matthäus** (c. 1487). *Geometria Deutsch.* Regensburg.

.. _cite-vitruvius-architectura:

- **Vitruvius Pollio, Marcus** (c. 25 BC). *De Architectura Libri Decem* (Books III, IX & X). Rome.
