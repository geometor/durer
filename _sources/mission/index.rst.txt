=======
Mission
=======

Our mission in **GEOMETOR • durer** is threefold:

1. **Restore Dürer's Vernacular Mathematical Voice**: Pair the authentic 1525 Nuremberg Early
   New High German copy-text (`German (1525)`) side-by-side with a crisp, faithful English
   translation (`English`), structural digests (`Summary`), and mathematical commentary (`Notes`),
   illuminating how Dürer forged German technical prose (*Eierlini*, *Brennspiegellini*,
   *Gabellini*, *Schneckenlini*, *Muschellini*, *Barlini*, *Fierung*, *Durchsehung*).
2. **Honor Dürer's Epistemic Honesty (*Demonstrative* vs. *Mechanice*)**: Dürer was among the
   earliest Renaissance authors to draw an explicit line between exact Euclidean proof
   (*demonstrative*) and rapid workshop approximation (*mechanice*). Using ``geometor.model``
   and ``SymPy``, we prove both sides of his distinction with exact symbolic algebra—computing
   the irreducible cubic residual $P_7(\frac{3}{4}) = -\frac{1}{64}$ of his heptagon, the nested
   quadratic radicals of his $+21.97'$ single-aperture pentagon, and the exact bilateral symmetry
   $w_k = \frac{\sqrt{2k(12-k)}}{3}$ of his two-view conic ellipse.
3. **Unify Constructive Geometry Across Centuries**: Connect Dürer's 1525 Nuremberg workshop
   directly to sister GEOMETOR editions—``euclid`` / ``elements``, ``pacioli``, ``pappus``,
   ``plato``, ``lemoine``, ``hilbert``, ``thompson``, and ``divine``.
