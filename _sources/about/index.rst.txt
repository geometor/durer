=====
About
=====

**GEOMETOR • durer** is the scholarly Digital Scriptorium edition and exact symbolic engine for
**Albrecht Dürer's** *Underweysung der Messung, mit dem Zirckel un[d] Richtscheyt, in Linien
Ebnen unnd gantzen Corporen* (Nuremberg, 1525; 2nd ed. 1538), collated with **Joachim
Camerarius's** 1532 Paris Latin translation *Institutionum Geometricarum Libri Quatuor*.

Historical Significance
=======================

Published in Nuremberg in 1525 and dedicated to his lifelong humanist friend **Willibald
Pirckheimer**, Dürer's *Underweysung der Messung* is the **first printed mathematics book in the
German language** and the decisive bridge between two worlds:

1. **Classical Greek Deductive Geometry** (*demonstrative*): Euclid's *Elements*, Archimedes's
   spirals and semi-regular polyhedra, Apollonius's conic sections, Nicomedes's conchoid, and
   Plato/Sporus/Hero's solutions to the Delian doubling of the cube.
2. **Northern & Italian Workshop Practice** (*mechanice*): Medieval stonemasons' single-aperture
   compass traditions (*Geometria Deutsch*, Matthäus Roriczer, Abū al-Wafāʾ), Luca Pacioli and
   Leonardo da Vinci's *Divina Proportione* (1509), Leon Battista Alberti and Piero della
   Francesca's linear perspective, Vitruvian architecture and gnomonics, and Nuremberg punchcutting
   and calligraphy.

Archival & Translation Integrity
================================

- **German (1525) Track**: Diplomatic Early New High German transcription of the 1525 Nuremberg
  *editio princeps* held by SLUB Dresden (`PPN 27778509X`), preserving original folio pagination,
  orthographic superscripts (`uᵒ`), and Dürer's own 1525 Errata.
- **English Track**: Fresh, independent GEOMETOR English translation prepared directly from
  Dürer's 1525 Nuremberg German and collated against Camerarius's 1532 Latin terminology.
- **Exact Symbolic Engine**: Every geometric construction in ``geometor.durer`` preserves 100%
  exact algebraic coordinates in ``SymPy`` and ``geometor.model`` without floating-point
  injection.
