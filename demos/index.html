Exact Symbolic Demonstrations (demos/)¶

All executable Python constructions for Albrecht Dürer’s Underweysung der Messung live cleanly outside the historical codex in demos/ and src/geometor/durer/. Every Model instance runs with auto_intersect=False and 100% exact SymPy coordinates over $\mathbb{Q}$, $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2})$, $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3})$, $\mathbb{Q}(\phi)$, and $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5}, \sin 36^\circ)$.

Constructs Dürer’s 12-sector Archimedean spiral (Schneckenlini) over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3})$ and verifies his 12-station descriptive-geometry transfer law $w_k = \frac{\sqrt{2k(12 - k)}}{3}$ for the right circular cone, proving exact bilateral symmetry $w_{12-k} = w_k$ (Elipsis vs. Eierlini).
