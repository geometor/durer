geometor.durer¶

GEOMETOR Dürer: Exact symbolic constructions & bilingual codex for Underweysung der Messung (1525).

Provides 100% exact SymPy + geometor.model constructions across all Four Books (Vier Bücher) of Albrecht Dürer’s Underweysung der Messung, mit dem Zirckel un[d] Richtscheyt, in Linien Ebnen unnd gantzen Corporen (Nuremberg, 1525; 2nd ed. 1538; Camerarius Latin ed. 1532):

  • Book I (geometor.durer.conics_perspective): 12-sector Archimedean spirals (Schneckenlinien) and two-view descriptive geometry of conic sections (Elipsis / Eierlini, Parabola / Brennspiegellini, Hiperbole / Gabellini).

  • Book II (geometor.durer.polygons): Exact comparison of Dürer’s Single-Aperture (“Rusty Compass”) Pentagon with Euclid’s Q(phi) regular pentagon, plus cubic-impossibility and quadrature audits for workshop 7-, 11-, and 13-gons and pi = 25/8.

  • Book III (geometor.durer.typography): Compass-and-straightedge Roman Antiqua capitals (A, O, Q) in the 10 x 10 square and modular Gothic Textura minuscules.

  • Book IV (geometor.durer.polyhedra, geometor.durer.conics_perspective): Planar fold-out polyhedral nets for Platonic & Archimedean solids, Delian cube duplication, and central perspective projection onto the glass picture plane.

geometor.durer.construct_durer_perspective_apparatus(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → PerspectiveApparatusResult¶

Project a 3D cube in Q^3 onto Dürer’s picture plane x = 0 from eye E(-12, 3, 5).

Verifies:

  1. Exact rational screen coordinates (y', z') in Q^2 for all 8 cube vertices.

  2. Exact convergence of all 4 depth edges at principal vanishing point V_x = (3, 5).

  3. Exact projective cross-ratio invariance CR(P4', P8'; P6', V_x) = -1 (harmonic range!).

  4. Exact algebraic verification of Plato’s / Sporus’s two mean proportionals (Book IV, Fig. 50) for doubling the cube (g = 1, e = 2 => c = 2^(1/3), d = 2^(2/3), c^3 = 2).

geometor.durer.construct_durer_spiral_volute(turns: int = 2, model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → SpiralVoluteResult¶

Construct Dürer’s Book I Archimedean spiral over Q(sqrt(3)) using 12 angular sectors.

Dürer divides the outer circle into 12 radial rays (1..12, every 30°) and divides the outer radius R = 12 into 12 * turns equal radial steps r_k = k / turns, plotting P_k = (r_k * cos(k * 30°), r_k * sin(k * 30°)) with 100% exact coordinates in Q(sqrt(3)).

geometor.durer.construct_durer_heptagon_workshop(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → HeptagonWorkshopReport¶

Construct Dürer’s workshop regular heptagon in the unit circle and audit its cubic residual.

In Book II, Figs. 11 & 15, Dürer sets the heptagon chord equal to the half-side of the inscribed equilateral triangle: s_7 = sqrt(3)/2 * R (Q_Durer = 3/4 for R = 1). The true regular heptagon chord quadrance Q_7 = 2*(1 - cos(2*pi/7)) is a root of the Eisenstein-irreducible cubic P_7(q) = q^3 - 7*q^2 + 14*q - 7 = 0, yielding the exact non-zero rational residual P_7(3/4) = -1/64.

geometor.durer.construct_durer_textura_minuscules(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → TexturaMinusculesResult¶

Construct Dürer’s modular Textura minuscules i, n, and m from unit squares.

In Book III (p. 136), Dürer constructs i first because nearly the entire Gothic minuscule alphabet is generated combinatorially from its modular stem. For n (two stems) and m (three stems), adjacent stems touch corner-to-corner at their 45° diamond caps! Wait: look at the spacing when adjacent top diamonds touch corner-to-corner: Top diamond of stem 1 has right corner at x0 + 1/3 + sqrt(2)/2 and left corner at x0 + 1/3 - sqrt(2)/2, so corner-to-corner contact spaces consecutive stems by exact pitch sqrt(2) (leaving interior counter gap sqrt(2) - 1 ≈ 0.414 < 1, matching Dürer’s exact words: “so wirt das felt schlemer zwischen den zügen dann ein zug des pustaben preyt ist”!).
