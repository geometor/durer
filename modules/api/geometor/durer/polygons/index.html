geometor.durer.polygons¶

Exact symbolic constructions & workshop audits for Book II regular polygons and quadrature.

Covers Albrecht Dürer’s Underweysung der Messung (1525), Book II:

  • Figs. 15 & 16 (pp. 058–059): Direct symbolic comparison between:

    1. Euclid / Ptolemy’s exact regular pentagon over Q(phi) (phi = (1 + sqrt(5))/2), marked demonstrative by Dürer, with exact interior angle 108° and diagonal-to-side ratio phi.

    2. Dürer’s Single-Aperture (“Rusty Compass”) Pentagon (mit unverrucktem Zirckel, inherited from Abū al-Wafāʾ and Geometria Deutsch), which constructs all five sides with exact unit quadrance Q = 1 and exact bilateral symmetry using a single fixed compass opening, but yields nested quadratic-radical vertices whose base interior angle is ~108.3660° (+0.3660°, or ~21.96' arcminutes too wide).

  • Figs. 11 & 15 (pp. 057–059): Dürer’s workshop regular heptagon (7-gon), taking half the side of the inscribed equilateral triangle s_7 = (sqrt(3)/2) * R (Q = 3/4 * R^2), audited against the irreducible cubic minimal polynomial P_7(q) = q^3 - 7*q^2 + 14*q - 7 (Eisenstein at p = 7), with exact rational residual P_7(3/4) = -1/64.

  • Fig. 19 (pp. 060–061): Workshop mechanice rules for the 11-gon (s_11 = 9/16 * R) and 13-gon (s_13 = 1/2 * R).

  • Fig. 34 (p. 075): Workshop quadrature of the circle (square diagonal 10 parts, circle diameter 8 parts, implying pi_Durer = 25/8 = 3.125).

geometor.durer.polygons.construct_durer_heptagon_workshop(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → HeptagonWorkshopReport¶

Construct Dürer’s workshop regular heptagon in the unit circle and audit its cubic residual.

In Book II, Figs. 11 & 15, Dürer sets the heptagon chord equal to the half-side of the inscribed equilateral triangle: s_7 = sqrt(3)/2 * R (Q_Durer = 3/4 for R = 1). The true regular heptagon chord quadrance Q_7 = 2*(1 - cos(2*pi/7)) is a root of the Eisenstein-irreducible cubic P_7(q) = q^3 - 7*q^2 + 14*q - 7 = 0, yielding the exact non-zero rational residual P_7(3/4) = -1/64.
