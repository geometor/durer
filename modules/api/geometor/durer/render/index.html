geometor.durer.render¶

Theme-adaptive .euclid-graphic SVG renderer & figure generator for geometor.durer.

Serializes exact Model constructions from Books I–IV of Albrecht Dürer’s Underweysung der Messung (1525) into interactive, publication-grade .euclid-graphic SVGs compatible with photon-platform/publish’s .. picture:: directive and :g: hover roles.
