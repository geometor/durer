geometor.durer.polyhedra¶

Exact symbolic planar polyhedral nets from Book IV of Dürer’s Underweysung der Messung (1525).

In Book IV (pp. 142–158, Figs. 29–43), Albrecht Dürer published the first printed fold-out planar polyhedral nets in the history of mathematics (aufgeschnittene und nidergelegte Corpora), covering all 5 Platonic solids, 7 Archimedean semi-regular solids, and 2 invented composite solids.

Every net constructed in this module uses 100% exact symbolic SymPy coordinates with unit edge quadrance Q = 1:

  • Tetrahedron (Fig. 29), Octahedron (Fig. 30), Icosahedron (Fig. 31), and Cuboctahedron (Fig. 37): coordinates in Q(sqrt(3)).

  • Cube (Fig. 32): coordinates in Q.

  • Dodecahedron (Fig. 33 — two 6-pentagon rosettes hinged across a shared outer petal edge): coordinates in the degree-4 cyclotomic subfield Q(sqrt(5), sin(36°)) where sin(36°) = sqrt(10 - 2*sqrt(5)) / 4 and phi = (1 + sqrt(5)) / 2.
