geometor.durer.conics_perspective¶

Exact symbolic constructions for Book I spirals & conic projections and Book IV perspective.

Covers two landmark innovations in Albrecht Dürer’s Underweysung der Messung (1525):

  1. Book I — Spirals & Two-View Descriptive Geometry of Conic Sections (pp. 011, 018, 034–039):

    • construct_durer_spiral_volute: Exact 12-sector Archimedean spiral (Schneckenlini, Book I, Figs. 7 & 17) over Q(sqrt(3)).

    • construct_durer_conic_sections: Dürer’s orthographic elevation (Aufriss) + circular ground plan (Grundriss) + rotated true-shape section plane for the right circular cone (radius R = 6, height H = 12), proving algebraically that his 12-station transfer algorithm yields w_k^2 = (2/9)*k*(12 - k)—identically symmetric (w_{12-k} = w_k), confirming a true Apollonian ellipse rather than an asymmetric Eierlini (egg-curve)—alongside exact focal and asymptotic laws for the Parabola / Brennspiegellini and Hiperbole / Gabellini.

  2. Book IV — Central Perspective Apparatus & Delian Cube Duplication (pp. 159–181):

    • construct_durer_perspective_apparatus: Exact homogeneous ray-plane intersection over Q from eye point E = (-12, 3, 5) onto picture plane x = 0 for cube [4, 8] x [-2, 2] x [0, 4], verifying convergence at principal vanishing point V_x = (3, 5), harmonic cross-ratio invariance CR(P4', P8'; P6', V_x) = -1, and Plato’s two-mean-proportionals identity c^3 = g^2 * e for doubling the cube.

geometor.durer.conics_perspective.construct_durer_spiral_volute(turns: int = 2, model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → SpiralVoluteResult¶

Construct Dürer’s Book I Archimedean spiral over Q(sqrt(3)) using 12 angular sectors.

Dürer divides the outer circle into 12 radial rays (1..12, every 30°) and divides the outer radius R = 12 into 12 * turns equal radial steps r_k = k / turns, plotting P_k = (r_k * cos(k * 30°), r_k * sin(k * 30°)) with 100% exact coordinates in Q(sqrt(3)).

geometor.durer.conics_perspective.construct_durer_conic_sections(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → ConicSectionsResult¶

Construct Dürer’s Book I elevation + ground-plan + true-section diagram for the ellipse.

Also computes exact focal and asymptotic invariants for Dürer’s Parabola (Brennspiegellini) and Hiperbole (Gabellini).

geometor.durer.conics_perspective.construct_durer_perspective_apparatus(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → PerspectiveApparatusResult¶

Project a 3D cube in Q^3 onto Dürer’s picture plane x = 0 from eye E(-12, 3, 5).

Verifies:

  1. Exact rational screen coordinates (y', z') in Q^2 for all 8 cube vertices.

  2. Exact convergence of all 4 depth edges at principal vanishing point V_x = (3, 5).

  3. Exact projective cross-ratio invariance CR(P4', P8'; P6', V_x) = -1 (harmonic range!).

  4. Exact algebraic verification of Plato’s / Sporus’s two mean proportionals (Book IV, Fig. 50) for doubling the cube (g = 1, e = 2 => c = 2^(1/3), d = 2^(2/3), c^3 = 2).
