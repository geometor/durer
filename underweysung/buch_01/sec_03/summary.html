Summary — Planar Spirals, Volutes & Spatial Helices (Figs. 6–27)¶

  1. Planar Spirals, Projective Volutes & Spatial Helices (pp. 010–029, Figs. 6–27):

    • Constructs multi-arc compass spirals (Fig. 6), the exact 12-sector Archimedean spiral $r = a\theta$ (Figs. 7 & 17), projectively accelerated spirals via circular-arc ray pencils (Figs. 8–9), spatial cylindrical and conical helices (Schrauffenlinien, Figs. 12–15), Vitruvian Ionic volutes (Fig. 24), and the infinite self-similar logarithmic spiral (Fig. 27).
