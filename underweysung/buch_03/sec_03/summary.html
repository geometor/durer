Summary — Roman Antiqua Capitals & Modular Gothic Textura¶

  1. Constructive Typography: Roman *Antiqua* & Modular *Textura* (pp. 115–141):

    • Constructs A–Z in the $10 \times 10$ (and $9 \times 9$) rechte Fierung with $1 : frac{1}{3}$ stroke contrast, tangent circular serif fillets (Ausschweiffung), and two-circle shifted-diagonal bowls (O, Q, C, D, G).

    • Builds Gothic Textura minuscules combinatorially from the foundational i-stem (3 unit squares + 2 $45^\circ$ Ort-Fierungen), proving the $\sqrt{2}$ stem pitch of n and m.
