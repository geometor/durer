Summary — Plane Enclosure, Corners vs. Angles & Quadrilaterals (Figs. 1–8)
==========================================================================

1. **Plane Enclosure, Angles & Quadrilateral Taxonomy (pp. 053–056, Figs. 1–8)**:

   - Establishes why two straight lines cannot bound a planar region (Euclid I, Def. 19), whereas
     lenses and crescents (*Monschein*) of two arcs do.
   - Distinguishes salient exterior corners (*Eck*) from recessed interior angles (*Winckel*),
     constructs right/acute/obtuse angles via Thales's semicircle, and classifies triangles and
     the eight quadrilateral types (``a``–``h``).
