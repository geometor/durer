Summary — Gothic Tracery, Planar Tilings & Pentagonal Parquetries (Figs. 21–27)
================================================================================

3. **Circle Packings, Gothic Tracery & Pentagonal Parquetries (pp. 061–071, Figs. 21–27)**:

   - Contrasts 4-neighbor square circle packing with 6-neighbor hexagonal packing (*6 touch the
     7th*) and 12-fold Gothic tracery stars (Fig. 21).
   - Investigates periodic and locally fivefold planar tilings of ``3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8``-gons,
     including four pentagonal parquetries (*fünf rosen*) with golden rhombic interstices (Fig. 24)
     and the Archimedean ``4.8.8`` octagon-square tiling (Fig. 26).
