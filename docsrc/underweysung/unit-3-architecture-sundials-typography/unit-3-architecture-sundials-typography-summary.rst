Summary — Book III: Architecture, Sundials, Scaling & Typography
==========================================================================

Structural Overview (SLUB N1525 pp. 077–141, Figs. 1–27 + Alphabets)
====================================================================

1. **Architectural Solids, Moldings, Entasis & Twisted Columns (pp. 077–107, Figs. 1–19)**:

   - Generates cylindrical, polygonal, and twisted shafts (*Seülen*), upright and oblique cones /
     pyramids (*Piramides*), and Late Gothic rib-vault pier sections (*Pfeyler*) by coupling ground
     plans to elevations.
   - Praises :person:`Vitruvius <vitruvius>`, codifies six elementary lathe/plane molding curves
     (*blatte Fasen, Holkelen, Wellen, Eck, Winckel, Schlangenlini*), gives two geometric rules for
     column entasis, and envelopes spheres along a 60-station spatial helix to draw serpentine
     columns, memorial monuments, and spiral market-tower ramps.

2. **Nuremberg Gnomonics ($49^\circ$ Latitude) & Optical Scaling (pp. 108–114, Figs. 20–27)**:

   - Divides the quadrant into $90^\circ = 3 \times 3 \times 2 \times 5$ and sets Nuremberg's polar
     elevation $\varphi = 49^\circ$ (equinoctial complement $41^\circ$) to construct equatorial,
     vertical South-wall, horizontal, and truncated-cube polyhedral sundials.
   - Projects equal visual angles from eye point ``c`` across a circular arc onto high walls so
     monumental inscriptions and statues read uniformly from ground level.

3. **Constructive Typography: Roman *Antiqua* & Modular *Textura* (pp. 115–141)**:

   - Constructs ``A``–``Z`` in the $10 \times 10$ (and $9 \times 9$) *rechte Fierung* with $1 :
     \frac{1}{3}$ stroke contrast, tangent circular serif fillets (*Ausschweiffung*), and two-circle
     shifted-diagonal bowls (``O, Q, C, D, G``).
   - Builds Gothic *Textura* minuscules combinatorially from the foundational ``i``-stem (3 unit
     squares + 2 $45^\circ$ *Ort-Fierungen*), proving the $\sqrt{2}$ stem pitch of ``n`` and ``m``.
