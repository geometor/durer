==========================================================================
*Underweysung der Messung* (Nuremberg, 1525 / Camerarius, 1532)
==========================================================================

.. epigraph::

   *Dem meinen insonders lieben Herren vnd freünd / Herren Wilibalden Pirckheymer entbeüt ich
   Albrecht Dürer mein willig dienst. ... Dann aus der messung mag man allerley gestalt vnnd
   ding für sich nemen zu machen.*

   — **Albrecht Dürer**, Dedication to :person:`Willibald Pirckheimer <pirckheimer>`, Nuremberg, 1525

Four-track bilingual Digital Scriptorium edition of **Albrecht Dürer's** *Underweysung der
Messung, mit dem Zirckel un[d] Richtscheyt, in Linien Ebnen unnd gantzen Corporen*
(:cite:`durer:1525`), pairing a fresh GEOMETOR English translation (`English`) directly from the
1525 Nuremberg Early New High German *editio princeps* (`SLUB Dresden PPN 27778509X`) with the
diplomatic 1525 German copy-text (`German (1525)`), structural digests (`Summary`), and exact
algebraic and historical commentary (`Notes`) collated against :person:`Joachim Camerarius's <camerarius>`
1532 Paris Latin edition (:cite:`durer:1532:camerarius`) and Dürer's posthumous 1538 Nuremberg
second edition (:cite:`durer:1538`).
