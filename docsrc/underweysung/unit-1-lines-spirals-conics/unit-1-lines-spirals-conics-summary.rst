==========================================================================
Summary — Book I: Dedication, Lines, Spirals & Conic Sections
==========================================================================

Structural Overview (SLUB N1525 pp. 005–052, Figs. 1–50)
========================================================

1. **Dedication to Willibald Pirckheimer (pp. 005–006)**:

   - Diagnoses the central weakness of Northern European workshop training: technical dexterity
     without geometric demonstration (*wie eyn wylder vnbeschnytener bawm*).
   - Defends visual art against Reformation iconoclasm and situates *die Kunst der Messung* as
     the shared rational foundation of painting, sculpture, goldsmithing, masonry, and carpentry.

2. **Dimensional Hierarchy & Elementary Curves (pp. 007–009, Figs. 1–5)**:

   - Translates Euclidean definitions (:term:`Punct <punct>`, *Lini*, *Ebne / Planum*, *Corpus*)
     into German for the first time in print.
   - Classifies three generative curves: straight line (*gerade Lini*), circle (*Zirckellini*),
     and inflected serpentine curve (:term:`Schlangenlini <schlangenlini>`), plus generalized
     equidistant parallels (:term:`Barlini <barlini>`) and the spherical convergence of plumb lines.

3. **Planar Spirals, Projective Volutes & Spatial Helices (pp. 010–029, Figs. 6–27)**:

   - Constructs multi-arc compass spirals (Fig. 6), the exact 12-sector Archimedean spiral
     $r = a\theta$ (Figs. 7 & 17), projectively accelerated spirals via circular-arc ray pencils
     (Figs. 8–9), spatial cylindrical and conical helices (*Schrauffenlinien*, Figs. 12–15),
     Vitruvian Ionic volutes (Fig. 24), and the infinite self-similar logarithmic spiral (Fig. 27).

4. **Invention of Two-View Descriptive Geometry for Conic Sections (pp. 034–039, Figs. 34–37)**:

   - Couples vertical elevation (*aufgezogner Grund*) and horizontal plan (*nidergedruckter Grund*)
     via perpendicular projectors to plot the true shapes of Apollonius's three conic sections:
     *Elipsis* (*Eierlini*), *Parabola* (*Brennspiegellini* and paraboloidal burning mirror focus),
     and *Hiperbole* (*Gabellini*).

5. **Kinematic Linkages & Proportional Scales (pp. 039–052, Figs. 38–50)**:

   - Sliding-lance *Muschellini* (conchoid variant, Figs. 38–39), epitrochoidal *Spinnenlini*
     (limaçon, Fig. 40), four-jointed $\sqrt{2}$-scaled spatial curve-drawing arm (Figs. 41–42),
     and arithmetic/geometric/harmonic segment scales (Figs. 43–50).
