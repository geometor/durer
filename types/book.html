Book I: Lines, Spirals & Conic Sections

Dedication to Willibald Pirckheimer, foundational geometric definitions, Archimedean spirals, volutes, and the first printed two-view descriptive geometry of conic sections.

Albrecht Dürer

Instruction in Measurement

“To my especially dear Lord and friend, Lord Willibald Pirckheimer, I, Albrecht Dürer, offer my willing service. … For from measurement one may undertake to make all manner of forms and things.”

—Albrecht Dürer, Dedication to Willibald Pirckheimer, Nuremberg, 1525

English · German
Albrecht Dürer

Book II: Regular Polygons & Planar Tilings

Epistemology of demonstrative vs. mechanice regular polygons, fixed-aperture rusty-compass pentagon, pentagonal parquetries (fünf rosen), and circle quadrature.

Albrecht Dürer

Book III: Columns, Sundials & Roman/Gothic Typography

Vitruvian columns, 60-station enveloped-sphere helices, Nuremberg gnomonics for 49° latitude, optical tower scaling, Roman Antiqua A–Z, and modular Gothic Textura.

Albrecht Dürer

Book IV: Platonic & Archimedean Polyhedral Nets & Perspective

First printed planar fold-out nets for Platonic and Archimedean polyhedra, Delian cube duplication, two-view cube sciagraphy, and glass-frame central projection.

Albrecht Dürer
