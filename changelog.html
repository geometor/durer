  • Exact Symbolic Library (src/geometor/durer/):

    • polygons.py: construct_durer_vs_euclid_pentagon and construct_durer_heptagon_workshop.

    • polyhedra.py: construct_durer_polyhedral_nets and polyhedral_net_to_model across 6 Platonic and Archimedean solids.

    • typography.py: construct_durer_roman_letter_A, construct_durer_roman_letter_O, construct_durer_roman_letter_Q, and construct_durer_textura_minuscules.

    • conics_perspective.py: construct_durer_spiral_volute, construct_durer_conic_sections, and construct_durer_perspective_apparatus.

    • render.py, concordance.py, sphinx_ext.py, and cli.py.

  • 11 `.euclid-graphic` SVG Plates, 5 JSON apparatus registries in resources/underweysung/, 4 runnable demos/, and full pytest + ruff + publish build verification.
